The Horus Heresy

In this article we will go over the Horus Heresy, the exposition, key events, and important characters.

"So... what is The Horus Heresy"

The Horus Heresy is a story about a father (Emperor of Mankind) and his sons (The Primarchs) and his favourite son, Horus Lupercal, eventually turned against him. Thousands of years before the betrayal of Horus to the Emperor, humanity once had a massive interstellar civilization known as the Dark Age of Technology.

The Collapse

After the humans spread across the galaxy and developed technology, humanity's civilization collapsed. Then we have "The Men of Iron" which where, machines of artificial intelligence that eventually rebelled against humanity. Around the same time, there were violent warp storms that cut planets off from each other.The humans went without trade and communication which caused many human civilizations to collapse, slow down, or become completely different cultures. Humanity entered the dark period known as the Age of Strife.

Unification of Terra

The Emperor lauched the Unification Wars, and in doing so; therefore conquered the various warlords, techno-barbarians, and kingdoms fighting across Terra. The goal for the Emperor was to build a galaxy-wide civilization. The Emperor wanted to strengthen humanity; therefore, he needed an army. He began creating the foundations of the Imperium.

The Creation of Primarchs

The Primarchs were twenty genetically engineered, superhuman "sons" of the Emperor of Mankind. These "sons" served as military generals and split into opposing factions during the galaxy-wide civil war known as the Horus Heresy. Out of the twenty original Primarchs, two were lost or erased from history before the conflict. This left eighteen active leaders who chose either loyalty to the Emperor or "allegiance" to the Warmaster Horus. The Traitor Primarchs are comprised of nine brothers that joined Warmaster Horus in turning against the Imperium and serving the Chaos Gods, these include: Horus Lupercal, Primarch of the Sons of Horus and Warmaster who led the rebellion. Fulgrim, Primarch of the Emperor's Children, consumed by a search for physical and sensory perfection. Perturabo, Primarch of the Iron Warriors, he is described as being: bitter, cold calulation, and a genius at siege breaking and engineering. Konrad Curze, Primarch of the Night Lords, he is "terrifying, sadistic, and driven by a warped, fatalistic sense of justice." Angron, Primarch of the World Eaters, Raging, endlessly wrathful, and is essentially broken by the Butcher's Nails in his brain. Mortarion, Primarch of the Death Guard, said to be resilient, grim, and depply hateful of sorcery and weakness. Magnus the Red, Primarch of the Thousand Sons, he is a cosmic psyker, scholar of the occult, and tragically hubristic. Lorgar Aurelian, Primarch of the Word Bearers, he is Zealous, deeply spiritual, and the architect of the religious corruption behind the Heresy. Alpharius Omegon, Primarch of the Alpha Legion, Twin Primarchs who practiced deception, misdirection, and secrecy.

Then we have "The Loyalists": Lion El'Johnson, Primarch of the Dark Angels, and is said to be Pragmatic, ruthless, and master tactician. Jahatai Khan, Primarch of the White Scars, he is swift, fiercly independent, and a master of lightning warfare. Lemun Russ, Primarch of the Space Wolves, is said to be savage, loyal, and at the time was acting as the Emperor's executioner. Rogal Dorn, Primarch of the Imperial Fists, is said to be stoic, uncompromising, and a master of defense and fortification. Sanguinius, Primarch of the Blood Angels, and is said to be charismatic, he is a winged angel-like warrior possessing tragic foresight. Ferrus Magnus, Primarch of the Iron Hands, and he is unyielding, cold and a master of technology and metal craftsmanship. Roboute Guilliman, Primarchs of the Ultramarines, he is logical, brilliant at administration, and a master of logistics organizer. Vulkan, Primarch of the Salamanders, he was an Empathetic, enduring, and a master smith who could regenerate from death. Corvus Corax, Primarch of the Raven Guard, he was stealthy, freedom-loving, and a master of guerilla and shadow warfare. Then there are the Lost who consist of: The II (2nd) and XI (11th) Primarchs: Their records were completely expunged from Imperial history, and their fates remain unknown.

Then we at last have Duel

Horus fights his brother Sanguinius and kills him, then afterward, faces the emperor in single combat. The Emperor defeats Horus; however, this came at such a cost. After the duel the Emperor is placed by Loyal Forces onto a life-support machine called the Golden Throne. Here he remains motionless and silent for the next ten millennia.
